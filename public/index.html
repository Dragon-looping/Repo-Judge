Evaluation Methodology

Understand the difference between deterministic rules and AI advisory verdicts.

Rules Only

Deterministic checks run against the repository's metadata. Results are reproducible, verifiable, and require no AI model. Identical inputs always produce identical outputs.

  • Checks for README, license, CI, tests, code of conduct
  • Verifiable: pass/fail with explicit evidence strings
  • No model call — ever
  • Runs in under a second for most repositories
Rules + Gemma 4

All deterministic rule checks, plus Gemma 4 (open-weight) AI verdicts for each criterion. AI scores are advisory — they complement, never override, the deterministic results.

  • Gemma 4 scores 0–5 per criterion with reasoning and evidence
  • Open-weight model: no closed or proprietary AI
  • AI results may be partial; unscored criteria are clearly marked
  • Adds 20–60 seconds of evaluation time (model inference)

AI verdicts are advisory. Rule results are deterministic and verifiable. Judges should use their own judgment.
